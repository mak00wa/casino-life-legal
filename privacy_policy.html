3. Data stored on your device

The Game saves your virtual chip balance, player level, daily reward and daily challenge progress, purchased items (e.g. “No ads”), settings (sound, graphics, vibration, notifications) and your age confirmation locally on your device. This data is not sent to us and is deleted when you uninstall the Game. 4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

We use Google AdMob to show rewarded and full-screen ads. To deliver, measure and personalise ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information such as the device advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device and app information, and interactions with ads. Google acts under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): on first launch you are asked, through Google’s consent form, whether you agree to personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in the Game: Settings → Ad privacy. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Google → Ads).

5. In-app purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. We receive only confirmation that a purchase was made so that we can deliver the item. We do not receive or store your card or payment details. Virtual chips have no monetary value, cannot be exchanged for money or prizes and cannot be transferred to other players.

6. Notifications

If you allow it, the Game schedules local reminders on your device (e.g. that your daily reward is ready). They are created on your device only and no data is sent to us. You can turn them off in Settings → Notifications or in your Android settings.

7. Updates

The Game may check with Google Play whether a newer version is available and offer you to update. This check is performed by Google Play on your device.

8. Legal basis (GDPR)

Processing related to personalised advertising is based on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time (see section 4). Processing needed to deliver purchases is necessary to perform the contract with you. Local storage of game progress is necessary to provide the Game.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase and restrict processing of your data, to object, to data portability and to withdraw consent. Because we do not store your personal data on our servers, most requests concern data processed by Google – you can manage them in your Google account and advertising settings. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in Poland: the President of the Personal Data Protection Office, UODO).

10. Children

The Game is not directed to children and is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has used the Game, contact us and uninstalling the Game removes all locally stored data.

11. Security and retention

We do not operate servers that store your data. Data stored locally remains on your device until you uninstall the Game. Data processed by Google is retained according to Google’s policies.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always available at this address, with the effective date shown at the top.
